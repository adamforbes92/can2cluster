Coolant Gauge Output

Drive the MK2 coolant temperature gauge from one of the ULN2003 outputs (EML or EPC). Selecting an output here takes exclusive ownership of that pin — any Shift Light or DSG Park use of the same pin is cleared.

Idiot-light mode: at or above this coolant temperature the gauge is pegged and the cluster warning light is triggered. Below it the output stays off.
